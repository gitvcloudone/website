## What
When you create an environment or add hosts, you can now choose a supported VCF and ESX version combination, from the console or the CreateEnvironment API.

## Why it matters
It makes it easier to match the version you run on-prem during a migration, and to keep new hosts on the same build as the ones you already have.

## Who it's for
EVS customers matching an on-prem VCF version, or expanding an existing environment.
