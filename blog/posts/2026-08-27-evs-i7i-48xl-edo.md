## What
Two things landed around EVS's first birthday. The **i7i.metal-48xl** is a bigger host on 5th Gen Intel Xeon, so you can run more VMs on fewer hosts. AWS says i7i gives up to 23% better compute and more than 10% better price performance than i4i.

AWS also released the **EVS Deployment Orchestrator (EDO)** in the Solutions for Amazon EVS repo. You launch one CloudFormation stack and it builds the AWS networking, the hosts and VCF 9.0 or 9.1. A full build takes about 4 to 7 hours and runs unattended.

## Why it matters
The 48xl is double the 24xl, which helps big clusters and memory tiering designs fit inside the 32-host limit. For VCF 9 you install VCF yourself with the VCF Installer, so EDO is the easy way to get a repeatable, hands-off build.

## Who it's for
Larger EVS deployments, VCF 9 adopters, and teams that want builds they can repeat.
