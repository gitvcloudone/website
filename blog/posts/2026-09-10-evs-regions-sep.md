Amazon EVS added four more Regions: Asia Pacific (Osaka), Asia Pacific (Taipei), Europe (Spain) and Israel (Tel Aviv).

Count December's seven and July's three, and that's fourteen new Regions in under a year. EVS environments live in a single Availability Zone, so resilience against a Regional outage means a second environment in another Region. More Regions make it easier to find a DR pair that's close enough for low replication lag and still inside the same country or legal area.

Taipei and Tel Aviv also open EVS to customers with strict in-country requirements in Taiwan and Israel.
