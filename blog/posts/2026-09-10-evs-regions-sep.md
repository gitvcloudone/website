Amazon EVS added four more Regions: Asia Pacific (Osaka), Asia Pacific (Taipei), Europe (Spain) and Israel (Tel Aviv).

Add December's seven and July's three, and that's fourteen new Regions in a year. More Regions help with data residency, and with DR designs that need a second Region close by.
