## What
Broadcom shipped VCF 9.1.2 today. Most of it is aimed at getting AI and Kubernetes platforms up faster:

- An AI infrastructure quickstart: one vCenter plugin configures the cluster, turns on Supervisor and builds a VKS cluster.
- A VKS quickstart that gives you a working Supervisor and VKS cluster, plus a test environment with add-ons.
- Chained VKr upgrades. Pick the version you want and VCF works out the steps to get there.
- The GitOps service (Argo CD inside VCF) and vSAN's S3-compatible object storage are now GA. Both were tech previews in 9.1.1.

## Why it matters
The object storage piece is the one I'd pay attention to even if AI isn't on your roadmap. Backups, logs and cloud-native apps can use S3 buckets on vSAN, so you don't need a separate object store for them.

## Who it's for
Teams building private AI or Kubernetes platforms on VCF, and anyone who needs S3 storage on-prem.
