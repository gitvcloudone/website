## What
A fix release for VCF 9.0. Two changes in vCenter 9.0.2 stood out to me: certificates issued by the VMware Certificate Authority for vCenter and ESX now renew automatically before they expire, and Supervisor control plane backup is on by default.

## Why it matters
Expired certificates are a common cause of outages, so automatic renewal is a welcome change. If you're still on 9.0.0 or 9.0.1, this is the build to plan for.

## Who it's for
VCF 9.0 customers.
