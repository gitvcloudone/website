VCF 9.0.2 is a fix release, but two changes in vCenter 9.0.2 are worth knowing about. Certificates issued by the VMware Certificate Authority for vCenter and ESX now renew on their own before they expire, and Supervisor control plane backup is on by default.

Expired certificates cause more outages than they should, so automatic renewal is a welcome change. If you're still on 9.0.0 or 9.0.1, this is the build to plan for.
