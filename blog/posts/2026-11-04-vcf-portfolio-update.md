Broadcom announced a few changes to the VCF portfolio:

- **vSphere Express**, a new entry edition on vSphere 9 for up to 128 cores. It includes vMotion, HA, distributed switching and TPM security.
- Two new Advanced Services: **VMware Live Recovery** for disaster and cyber recovery, and **VMware Compliance and Configuration Management**.
- Disaster recovery built into VCF and VVF themselves, planned for the next release.

Express gives small sites and branch offices a supported starting point that can move up to VVF or VCF later. The native DR piece is the one I'll be watching, since it could change how people design and license recovery.
