## What
I've added three sizers to the site, one each for Amazon EVS, VMware Cloud on AWS and on-prem VCF. Drop in RVTools exports or type in workload totals and you get hosts, clusters and management overhead, with checks and an Excel export.

- **EVS Sizing:** hosts and clusters for Amazon EVS.
- **VMC Sizing:** SDDCs, clusters and hosts, with placement and DR per workload group, a topology diagram and a memory tiering estimate per cluster.
- **VCF Sizing:** fleets, instances, domains and clusters on your own hardware, plus licensing and add-on overhead.

## Why it matters
Everything runs in your browser, so customer data stays on your machine. The VMC and VCF sizers explain each default when you hover over it, and the Excel export keeps your settings so you can load the sizing again later. Use them for early numbers and to compare options, then confirm with the official tools.

## Who it's for
Architects and partners who need a quick, defensible first sizing.
