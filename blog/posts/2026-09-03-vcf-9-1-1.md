VCF 9.1.1 came out the same week as VMware Explore in Las Vegas, and most of what's new is AI:

- **Multi-Tenant Model Sharing** lets several business units use one model deployment while keeping their data separate.
- **AI Assistant for VCF** is a chat assistant inside VCF Operations, and you choose the model provider.
- GitOps and native object storage showed up as tech previews. Both went GA in 9.1.2.

For most infrastructure teams, 9.1.1 is simply the maintenance build to land on after 9.1. The AI features need VCF Private AI Services, so they matter most if private AI is already on your plan.
