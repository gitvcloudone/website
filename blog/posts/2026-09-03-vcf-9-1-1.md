VCF 9.1.1 shipped the same week as VMware Explore in Las Vegas. Most of what's new is aimed at private AI and platform teams.

**Multi-tenant model sharing.** In VCF Private AI Services, one AI model deployment can now be shared by several tenants or business units. Each gets controlled access to the model without seeing the others' data, and access policy is managed in one place. The point is cost: GPUs are expensive, and running a separate copy of the same model for every team wastes them.

**AI Assistant for VCF (tech preview).** An assistant inside VCF Operations that helps with diagnostics, for example finding the cause of ESX CPU or memory contention, or making sense of a pile of vMotion errors across vCenter. It can also help build management packs for third-party integrations.

**Tech previews:** a GitOps service (Argo CD built into VCF, managed from VCF Automation) and native object storage on vSAN. Both went GA in 9.1.2.

**For networking teams:** EVPN gets a more direct datapath. Transit gateways build VXLAN tunnels straight to the leaf switches, and NAT, load balancing and DHCP relay now work in that mode.

For most infrastructure teams, 9.1.1 is simply the maintenance build to land on after 9.1. The AI features need VCF Private AI Services, so they matter most if private AI is already on your plan.
