## What
This is the big one for EVS this year. You can now run VCF 9.0 and 9.1 in every Region where EVS is available. EVS sets up the hosts and VPC networking, and you install VCF with Broadcom's VCF Installer. VCF evaluation mode is supported too, which is handy for proofs of concept.

## Why it matters
VCF 9 brings memory tiering and the rest of the 9.x platform to AWS. Since the VCF install is now your job, have the installer steps and your VCF subscription sorted before the hosts are ready.

## Who it's for
Customers moving to VCF 9 who want to run it on AWS, and anyone who wants memory tiering on EVS.
