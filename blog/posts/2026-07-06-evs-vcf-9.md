This is the big one for Amazon EVS this year. You can now run VCF 9.0 and 9.1 in every Region where EVS is available. EVS sets up the hosts and VPC networking, and you install VCF with Broadcom's VCF Installer. VCF evaluation mode is supported too, which is handy for proofs of concept.

VCF 9 brings memory tiering and the rest of the 9.x platform to AWS, and that's what a lot of customers have been waiting for. The catch is that the VCF install is now your job, so have the installer steps and your VCF subscription sorted before the hosts are ready.
