## What
A smaller host than i4i on a newer CPU, aimed at better cost-performance for VMware workloads.

## Why it matters
VCF is subscribed per core, so fewer, faster cores per host can save on licensing as well as on hosts.

## Who it's for
EVS customers who want a smaller, newer host, or who are watching per-core costs.
