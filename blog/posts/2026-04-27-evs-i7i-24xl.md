Amazon EVS now supports i7i.metal-24xl, a smaller host than i4i on a newer CPU, aimed at better cost-performance for VMware workloads.

The size matters as much as the speed. VCF is subscribed per core, so fewer, faster cores per host can lower your licensing bill as well as your host count. If per-core cost is a big part of your business case, run the numbers on this one.
