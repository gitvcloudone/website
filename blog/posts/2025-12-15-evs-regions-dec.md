## What
Amazon EVS expanded to US West (N. California), Asia Pacific (Hyderabad), Asia Pacific (Malaysia), Canada West (Calgary), Europe (Milan), Mexico (Central) and South America (São Paulo).

## Who it's for
Anyone who needs VMware workloads in those places.
