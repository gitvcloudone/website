Amazon EVS is now in Asia Pacific (Seoul), Europe (Zurich) and Europe (Stockholm), with every existing feature, VCF 9.0 and 9.1 included.

All three matter for data residency. Korea, Switzerland and Sweden each have customers, often in financial services and the public sector, whose data has to stay in-country. Until now, those customers couldn't use EVS without moving data across a border. Before planning, check which host types are offered in each Region.
