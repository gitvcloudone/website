SDDC 1.26 moves VMware Cloud on AWS to vSphere 8.0 Update 3, and it brings a lot with it:

- vSAN ESA is GA, with RAID-5 from 3 hosts. It's the default for new i4i deployments, for non-stretched SDDCs only.
- Bring your own key: VM encryption with an external KMIP key management system.
- VM hardware version 21, with up to 256 disks per VM and NVMe for Windows Server Failover Clustering.
- VMware Tools 12.5.4.

ESA is the change that affects sizing. Every drive is capacity and RAID-5 starts at three hosts, so small clusters get noticeably more usable storage than they did on OSA. Stretched SDDCs stay on OSA, so keep that in mind if you're comparing designs. BYOK is the other one to note if your security team insists on holding its own keys.
