## What
SDDC 1.26 moves VMC to vSphere 8.0 Update 3 and brings a lot with it:

- vSAN ESA is GA, with RAID-5 from 3 hosts. It's the default for new i4i deployments, for non-stretched SDDCs only.
- Bring your own key: VM encryption with an external KMIP key management system.
- VM hardware version 21, with up to 256 disks per VM and NVMe for Windows Server Failover Clustering.
- VMware Tools 12.5.4.

## Why it matters
ESA changes how you size VMC. Every drive is capacity and RAID-5 starts at three hosts, so small clusters get more usable storage than they did on OSA. Stretched SDDCs stay on OSA.

## Who it's for
Every VMC customer, especially new i4i SDDCs and anyone who needs to manage their own encryption keys.
