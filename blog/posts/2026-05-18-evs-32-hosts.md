The host limit per Amazon EVS environment went from 16 to 32, through a service quota increase. You can run one big cluster or split the hosts across several.

Fewer, bigger environments mean fewer sets of management appliances to run and patch. If you've outgrown 16 hosts, or you're running several small environments that could be one, ask for the quota before you need it.
