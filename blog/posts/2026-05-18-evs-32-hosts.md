## What
The limit per environment went from 16 hosts to 32, through a service quota increase. You can run one big cluster or split the hosts across several.

## Why it matters
Fewer, bigger environments mean fewer sets of management appliances to run and patch. It's a quota, so ask for it before you need it.

## Who it's for
Anyone who has outgrown 16 hosts, or who runs several small environments that could be one.
