HCX 4.11.4 is a maintenance release for VMware Cloud on AWS. Like 4.11.3, it ships without the WAN optimization appliance.

WAN-OPT used to sit in an HCX service mesh and compress and dedupe migration traffic, which mattered on slow or expensive links. On a fast Direct Connect link it added little, and Broadcom has now retired it.

If a service mesh still includes WAN-OPT, remove it from the mesh and its compute profile before you upgrade. If you're mid-migration over a thin link, expect transfers to take longer without it, and plan migration waves around that.
