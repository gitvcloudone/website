## What
A maintenance release of HCX. Like 4.11.3 before it, there's no WAN optimization appliance.

## Why it matters
If any service mesh still has WAN-OPT appliances, you'll need to remove them before upgrading.

## Who it's for
Customers migrating to VMC with HCX.
