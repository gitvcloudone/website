## What
With SDDC 1.26v2, VMC supports i7i.metal-24xl for new and existing SDDCs in select Regions. It comes with host-to-host encryption, vSAN data-at-rest encryption and Intel TME memory encryption.

A few things to know: existing SDDCs need to be on 1.26v2 first, vSAN ESA is only for new SDDCs or new secondary clusters, hyper-threading is on by default (96 logical cores per host), and stretched clusters on i7i use vSAN OSA.

## Why it matters
It gives i3, i3en and i4i customers a newer host to move to, as well as an option for new SDDCs.

## Who it's for
VMC customers planning a host refresh, and new SDDC deployments.
