## What
Broadcom's December roundup covered a few changes:

- A redesigned console at vmc.broadcom.com.
- Stretched clusters can now scale down to 4 hosts (2 per AZ) or 2 hosts (1 per AZ), with some limits.
- HCX 4.11.3 drops WAN optimization and is supported until October 11, 2027.
- Better estimates for moving from i3.metal to i3en.metal or i4i.metal.

## Why it matters
Smaller stretched clusters make a multi-AZ SDDC cheaper to start with. And if you use HCX WAN-OPT, plan to remove it before upgrading.

## Who it's for
Customers on stretched clusters or HCX, and anyone still on i3.metal.
