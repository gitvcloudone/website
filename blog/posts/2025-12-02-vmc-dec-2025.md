Broadcom's December roundup for VMware Cloud on AWS had a few useful changes:

- A redesigned console at vmc.broadcom.com.
- Stretched clusters can now scale down to 4 hosts (2 per AZ) or 2 hosts (1 per AZ), with some limits.
- HCX 4.11.3 drops WAN optimization and is supported until October 11, 2027.
- Better estimates for moving from i3.metal to i3en.metal or i4i.metal.

The smaller stretched clusters are the part I like most. A multi-AZ SDDC is now a lot cheaper to start with. If you use HCX, check your service meshes for WAN-OPT before you upgrade, because it has to come out first. And if you're still on i3.metal, the new estimates make it easier to plan the move off it.
